Key terms in a personal injury case

Legal words get used quickly after an accident. These are a few you are likely to hear.

Tort
A legal term defining your claim. It is the first key legal term in personal injury cases.
Negligence
A legal term defining other people's wrongful actions. The basis of tort law is whether someone has done something to hurt you when they should have been more careful.
Wrongful Death
A legal action by survivors of a deceased individual. The loved one's death must have been caused by the wrongful actions of another party.
Workers' Compensation
Benefits given to workers who have been injured during the course of their employment, such as lost salary, medical treatment and job rehabilitation.
